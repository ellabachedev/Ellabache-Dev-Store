Description

  • Antibacterial treatment cream to help clear up pimples, breakouts, congestion ingrown hairs without drying the skin out
  • Combines antibacterial qualities with healing agents to help soothe and calm breakouts
  • Made in Australia. Formulated with no parabens or mineral oil
  • A universal cult classic product designed to help treat and heal breakouts

Benefits

  • Helps to reduce redness, calm and soothe skin while absorbing excess oil
  • Promotes healing, to restore skin clarity
  • Helps prevent drying skin around affected area
  • Anti-inflammatory benefits

How to use

  • Take a small amount, emulsify onto fingertips and apply to the affected areas on clean skin
  • Use twice daily. More can be applied at night for maximum healing assistance. During the day apply and blend into skin
  • Application can be to targeted pimple or apply as a mask to congested areas
  • Can be used as a thin, oil absorbing veil under make-up during the day

Therapist tips

  • Suitable for oily, acne prone and combination skin
  • Suitable to all skins with occasional breakouts and pimples
  • Can be used to draw out and assist the healing process of ingrown hair
  • For best results use with Intensive Balancing Lotion to purify the area prior to applying
